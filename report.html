AI Overview correction

Capture the exact query, date, language, country setting, answer and cited URLs. Identify the inaccurate claim, correct its source where possible, then submit specific feedback with evidence. Repeat the same query set to check whether the claim changes. [P04, P07]

Google says normal SEO practices apply to AI features and no special AI markup is required. A supporting page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet. Source owners can use noindex or snippet controls for their own pages; Aurant cannot apply them to another publisher’s site. Recrawling can take days to months. [P01]

The report does not contain a measured Google ranking or AI Overview baseline. Establishing that baseline is a first phase deliverable. No agency can directly edit Google’s generated answer.
